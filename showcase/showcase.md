# Facet

Seven stones. One beautiful chain reaction. Take hold of a gem, carry it onto its neighbor, and let go. Line up three or more of one kind to shatter them and let fresh stones tumble into place.

Every clear leaves pressure behind. The stones beside it crack a little deeper, until they become flawed. Touch a flawed stone with another clear and it joins the fracture for double the points. Save that pressure, shape the board around it, and spend it in a rushing chain.

Longer matches leave something precious. Four stones earn a brilliant that bursts through its neighbors. Crossing lines earn a star that sweeps its row and column. Five or more earn a prism: trade it with a stone to take every gem of that kind.

Chase the next level's target while each falling match climbs the chain multiplier. There is no ticking clock—just the next move, the fractures you have built, and the possibility hidden in the stones.
